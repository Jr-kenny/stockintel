The problem

Prices react to events. We find the events first.

A trader watching NVDA will not find its next move by searching “NVDA”. The move starts elsewhere: a hyperscaler announces a $10B AI buildout, TSMC guides capacity up, a power supplier wins a data-center contract. StockIntel investigates the world behind the ticker and turns what it finds into ranked market impact assessments with reasoning and verifiable sources.

  • what event could materially change a stock's value
  • who is economically exposed: customers, suppliers, infrastructure
  • when the impact window opens, and how fresh the signal is
  • what evidence proves each link, and what could invalidate it

The loop

World → Event → Exposure → Thesis → Market check

  1. Target. You name a ticker. Say NVDA.
  2. Fan out. StockIntel investigates everything around the asset: data-center builds, hyperscaler capex, TSMC, suppliers, power and cooling, hiring, filings, procurement, interviews, factory footage. Never the ticker directly.
  3. Event. A discovery surfaces: a hyperscale AI infrastructure expansion.
  4. Exposure. One event fans into many: GPUs → NVDA, memory → MU, servers → DELL, networking → AVGO. Each edge carries its evidence.
  5. Market check. Only then, via Binance Agent OS, the agent reads live price, volume and your position, and says whether the market has reacted yet.

The sequence matters: the thesis is formed independently, then contextualized against price. Never the reverse.

An analytical assessment, not a prediction. Every claim ships with its source links.
